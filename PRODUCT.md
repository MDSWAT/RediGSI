# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Product Purpose
RediGSI / Roma Community Map maps communities, entrepreneurs and partner clients, and compares their geographic overlap.

## Users
Public viewers, authorized viewers, GIS editors and administrators managing community and partner data across multiple countries. The user confirmed that modules, roles and community validation are the first delivery priority.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Preserve Romanian copy, Leaflet map and layer controls, community drawing and validation, search, CSV/Excel/GeoJSON import and export, SIRUTA matching, and overlap analysis. Existing Next.js and React implementation; server-confirmed Supabase/PostgreSQL storage for all records, country summaries and original CSV text. No runtime data-file storage or fallback. No invented records or claims.

## Brand Commitments
Preserve the Roma Community Map product name. The user requested a modern redesign using Impeccable.
The latest request supersedes the forest/yellow identity: modern SaaS styling, explicitly closer to Linear, with a white/light map. Use white panels, cool gray navigation, restrained teal actions, compact Host Grotesk typography and gently rounded controls. Preserve the full-screen working map, persistent left navigation and internally scrolling tools. Do not introduce a landing page, invented records, pricing or claims.

## Evidence on Hand
README.md, implemented workflows, and explicitly fictitious CSV examples under public/samples.

## Platform expansion
Four permission-aware modules: Map Explorer, Analytics, Data Management and Administration. Preserve the light Linear-inspired identity and Romanian UI. Roles and multiple attribute-based geographic grants are enforced by PostgreSQL RPCs, with restricted-by-default legacy records, separate public proposals, explicit privacy-aware publication, audit/version history and recoverable deletion. The first release includes baseline analytics and secured existing import/export workflows. Full geocoding, KML, clustering, direct geometry editing and advanced business analytics remain subsequent phases documented in README.md. Live activation requires the governance migration and initial administrator setup.
